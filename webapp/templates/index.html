📋 Operational Intelligence Framework & Decision Protocols

Core telemetry questions and rapid dispatch directives formulated from distributed satellite observation

Frame Question 1
Spatiotemporal Wildfire Dynamics
What is the monthly and diurnal temporal distribution of global fire hotspots, and when do peak thermal energy spikes occur?
Operational Decision: Global fire incidence peaks strongly between July and October (1.45M fires/month). High-altitude aerial surveillance patrols should scale by 300% during this window across Northern boreal zones.
Frame Question 2
Diurnal vs. Nocturnal Thermal Energetics
How does Fire Radiative Power (FRP) and flaming intensity vary between day and night satellite overpasses?
Operational Decision: Nighttime fires represent ~30% of detections but any nocturnal event with FRP > 30 MW indicates an aggressive crown fire that has breached the nightly humidity barrier, triggering immediate priority escalation.
Frame Question 3
Land Cover Source & Confidence Screening
What proportion of hotspots represent genuine wildfires versus industrial flaring or volcanic activity?
Operational Decision: 90.1% are vegetation fires, while 8.9% are static industrial gas flares. Deploying geographic masking filters prevents false emergency dispatch mobilizations to oil refineries and gas fields.
Frame Question 4
Thermal Radiative Correlation Analysis
How strongly do VIIRS spectral brightness temperatures (I-4 vs I-5) correlate with physical Fire Radiative Power (FRP)?
Operational Decision: Channel I-4 (4µm) brightness and the differential (Brightness - Bright_T31) exhibit a strong positive correlation (r = +0.658) with FRP, demonstrating that spectral thermal contrast is the premier physical predictor for fire energy release.
Frame Question 5
Extreme Wildfire Risk Clustering (WARD Framework)
Where are persistent extreme fire clusters concentrated, and how does this define the operational dispatch framework?
Operational Decision: The Wildfire Alert & Rapid Dispatch (WARD) Framework defines four triage levels: Tier 0 (≥50 MW) for instant water bombers, Tier 1 (20-50 MW) for ground brigades, Tier 2 (5-20 MW) for local ranger watch, and Tier 3 (<5 MW) for satellite monitoring.
